# Code of Conduct

Anicat is a small project maintained by one person in their spare time. This
document says what is expected of everyone who takes part in it: issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other space the project runs.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Anicat to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn
  from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission. This includes attempts to identify the
  developer (see [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md#developer-privacy)).
- Requests to host, link, or share copyrighted content. Anicat hosts nothing,
  and its issue tracker is not a place to trade sources.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces, and in public spaces when someone is
officially representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the
maintainer, [@bonkedbythonk](https://github.com/bonkedbythonk), or through
GitHub's [Report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature on the offending content. Every report is reviewed, and the privacy of
the reporter is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is found to be
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Depending on severity, the
response ranges from a private warning, to a temporary ban from interacting
with the project, to a permanent ban.
